3. CLEANING PROCESS
----------------------------------------
Rows after removing exact duplicate rows: 536641
Rows after removing cancelled invoices: 527390
Rows after removing non-positive quantities: 526054
Rows after removing non-positive prices: 524878
Missing descriptions were replaced with 'Unknown Product'.
Text fields were stripped of unnecessary leading/trailing spaces.
InvoiceDate was converted to datetime format.
Quantity, UnitPrice and CustomerID were converted to numeric types.
Rows with invalid Quantity, UnitPrice or InvoiceDate were removed.

4. FEATURE ENGINEERING
----------------------------------------
Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice
Created Year, Month, Month_Name, Day and Day_of_Week.

5. OUTLIER DETECTION
----------------------------------------
Method: Interquartile Range (IQR)
Quantity lower bound: -14.0000
Quantity upper bound: 26.0000
UnitPrice lower bound: -3.0700
UnitPrice upper bound: 8.4500
Revenue lower bound: -16.8000
Revenue upper bound: 38.4000
Outlier records identified: 81467
Outliers were flagged rather than automatically deleted from the main cleaned transaction dataset because extreme values may represent genuine wholesale transactions.

6. NORMALIZATION AND ENCODING
----------------------------------------
Quantity, UnitPrice and Revenue were normalized using Min-Max scaling for the model-ready dataset.
Country was converted into numerical features using one-hot encoding.
CustomerID was retained as an identifier for customer analysis and not treated as a continuous numerical feature.
